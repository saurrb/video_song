Jab main kaam karne baithu,
Mera toh mann nahi lagta,
Mann bole reels dekh zara,
Socha tha bas do minute,
Do ghante kaise beet gaye,
Jaane mujhko kya ho gaya,
Kaam pada hai saamne mere,
Mann mera phir bhi na maanta, ho
Kabhi phone dekha karu, kabhi scroll kiya karu,
Bin matlab ke reels mein time gawaaya karu,
Hai koi notification ya koi bahana hai,
Kuch jaane agar to itna ki phone hi deewana hai,
Re mann mera, maane na mann mera
