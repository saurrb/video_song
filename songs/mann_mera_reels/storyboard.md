# Mann Mera — reels versus work

Girl-led story in the established Hansi Ki Dhun style. Sixteen portrait shots. Start determined, drift into a tiny phone break, lose two hours, spiral through excuses, then end with the phone winning again. Every shot is cued to the recording rather than equal-duration cuts. Keep Roman Hindi lyrics as supplied.

## 1. ready_to_work
Girl eagerly sits at wooden desk, hands poised over mint-green laptop keyboard, open notebook and pencil, tall tidy stack of work papers, phone with lavender case lying face-down off to side. She looks determined. Peach home office, sky-blue curtains, small plant, round wall clock.

## 2. bored
Same girl slumps at same wooden desk with cheek in palm, bored half-closed eyes and tiny sigh cloud; mint laptop open, untouched blank notebook and pencil. Lavender phone on desk starts softly glowing. Same peach home office.

## 3. reels_temptation
Girl at desk glances irresistibly toward lavender phone; a small cute pink heart-shaped thought mascot whispers toward her ear and points to the glowing screen containing simple colorful video rectangles with no text. Girl looks tempted, laptop ignored.

## 4. two_minutes
Girl holds up two fingers with confident innocent smile while picking up lavender phone, promising just a tiny break. Small hourglass on desk nearly full, mint laptop and work papers waiting. Same peach room. No text.

## 5. two_hours
Girl freezes wide-eyed shocked at huge round clock whose hands and translucent motion trails indicate time racing, two ghosted earlier hand positions behind current time. Phone in other hand; untouched work, cold tea mug, warm late-afternoon light. Comically stunned.

## 6. what_happened
Girl at desk scratching her head with baffled expression, little question mark doodles circling her, lavender phone still glued to other hand, empty cold tea cup and work papers. Realization and sheepish embarrassment.

## 7. work_waiting
Girl looks tiny behind towering mountain of unfinished papers and open mint laptop on desk. She peeks at lavender phone under desk with guilty sideways smile. Cute paper sheets have tiny sad cartoon faces, no writing.

## 8. mind_wont_listen
Girl tries heroically to place lavender phone in a small open drawer while her own other hand reaches toward it. Imaginary pink heart-shaped thought mascot tugs phone back with tiny string, comical tug of war with herself. Same room.

## 9. phone_glance
Close medium view girl holding pencil above blank notebook but eyes dramatically swivel toward lavender phone perched upright beside mint laptop. Tiny mischievous smile, irresistibly distracted. Phone screen has colorful abstract video thumbnails.

## 10. endless_scroll
Girl absorbed in phone, index finger scrolling; a long curling ribbon of colorful rounded video thumbnail cards floats from phone upward like an endless scroll. Cards show simple cats, dancing stars and food illustrations, no logos or text. Desk in background.

## 11. time_wasted
Girl floating dreamily in whimsical spiral of tiny video cards and flying clock faces, hugging lavender phone while a neglected notebook floats away. Pastel peach/sky blue dreamy background, funny depiction of time disappearing. No words.

## 12. notification
Girl at desk jolts excitedly at a bright little bell icon and red notification dot floating above lavender phone. Hands were ready to type on mint laptop but one already reaches for phone. Cute excited surprised face.

## 13. excuse
Girl gives exaggerated innocent puppy eyes and shrugs, hiding lavender phone behind her back while pointing with free hand at an enormous blank checklist on laptop. Small pink thought-heart gives mischievous wink. Same peach home office, comedy excuse.

## 14. phone_deewana
Girl gazes dramatically at lavender smartphone seated on a tiny royal cushion with a little paper crown, pink hearts and golden rays around it. Mint laptop and work papers at edge look neglected. Ridiculous Bollywood romance parody starring phone, no boy.

## 15. mann_mera
Girl and cute pink heart-shaped thought mascot pull in opposite directions: girl reaches toward mint laptop while heart guides her toward lavender phone. She has melodramatic pleading eyes, tiny musical-note doodles. Same wooden desk peach room, funny inner struggle.

## 16. relapse
Final punchline callback to first desk: girl has proudly closed laptop and stacked notebook to focus, but immediately curls into chair smiling at lavender phone again. Giant pile of unfinished work right beside her, tiny clock and cold tea, warm evening light. Sheepish cheeky glance at viewer; phone wins. No words.
